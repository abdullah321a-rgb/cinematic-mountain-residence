

\# ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN

\## CINEMATIC MOUNTAIN RESIDENCE



\## 1. Architectural Overview



The project features a contemporary mountain residence with an open-plan interior.



The architectural concept focuses on spatial continuity, panoramic glazing and a strong connection with the surrounding landscape.



\## 2. Spatial Organization



The main interior consists of three connected zones.



\### Zone A - Kitchen



Location: Left side of the main composition.



Elements:

\- Kitchen cabinets.

\- Kitchen island.

\- Integrated appliances.

\- Storage systems.

\- Decorative lighting.



\### Zone B - Lounge Area



Location: Central part of the composition.



Elements:

\- Lounge furniture.

\- Coffee table.

\- Decorative objects.

\- Architectural accents.



\### Zone C - Living Room



Location: Right side of the composition.



Elements:

\- Main sofa.

\- Coffee table.

\- Suspended fireplace.

\- Additional seating.

\- Decorative lighting.



\## 3. Panoramic Glazing



Floor-to-ceiling windows are a major architectural feature.



Objectives:

\- Maximize mountain views.

\- Introduce natural daylight.

\- Visually connect interior and exterior.

\- Maintain clean architectural lines.



Exact dimensions and window configuration remain to be determined.



\## 4. Ceiling Design



The ceiling should complement the overall architectural composition.



Possible elements:

\- Integrated linear lighting.

\- Recessed lighting.

\- Architectural ceiling details.

\- Concealed lighting profiles.



Final geometry requires further development.



\## 5. Fireplace



The suspended fireplace serves as an important visual focal point.



Design direction:

\- Contemporary sculptural form.

\- Dark metallic finish.

\- Minimalist appearance.

\- Realistic fire illumination.



Exact model and dimensions remain open.



\## 6. Interior Geometry



The architectural model must prioritize:



\- Accurate proportions.

\- Clean geometry.

\- Correct wall thickness.

\- Realistic window openings.

\- Proper floor and ceiling construction.

\- Consistent object scale.



\## 7. Modeling Priorities



1\. Establish the main room geometry.

2\. Define the three functional zones.

3\. Model panoramic glazing.

4\. Develop ceiling architecture.

5\. Position the fireplace.

6\. Verify furniture circulation and proportions.



\## 8. Unconfirmed Parameters



The following information must be established before detailed modeling:



\- Total room length and width.

\- Ceiling height.

\- Wall thickness.

\- Window dimensions.

\- Window frame configuration.

\- Door and entrance location.

\- Exact fireplace dimensions.

\- Furniture dimensions.



No unconfirmed measurements should be treated as final architectural specifications.



\## 9. Architectural Objective



Create a coherent, realistic and visually balanced architectural environment suitable for high-end interior visualization.
