

\# VISUAL DIRECTION

\## CINEMATIC MOUNTAIN RESIDENCE



\## 1. Visual Identity



Style: Cinematic Modern Luxury



The visual identity combines contemporary luxury, natural materials and dramatic architectural lighting.



The interior should feel premium, atmospheric and visually balanced.



\## 2. Color Palette



| Color | HEX | Application |

|---|---|---|

| Deep Charcoal | #202020 | Architectural surfaces |

| Graphite | #30302E | Walls and details |

| Warm Taupe | #8B8175 | Neutral surfaces |

| Dark Walnut | #49372C | Wood furniture |

| Burgundy | #592B35 | Accent elements |

| Warm Light | #E8C99B | Lighting atmosphere |



\## 3. Material Character



Primary materials:



\- Dark natural walnut.

\- Textured natural stone.

\- Matte graphite surfaces.

\- Warm bronze metal.

\- Soft premium fabrics.

\- Transparent architectural glass.



Materials must have realistic roughness, reflections and surface imperfections.



\## 4. Lighting Direction



The lighting should create a cinematic atmosphere without losing architectural readability.



Main lighting elements:



\- Natural daylight through panoramic windows.

\- Warm indirect ceiling lighting.

\- Decorative accent lighting.

\- Fireplace illumination.

\- Soft furniture lighting.



Avoid excessive brightness and completely crushed shadows.



\## 5. Contrast and Color Balance



The overall image should maintain:



\- Deep but readable shadows.

\- Warm interior highlights.

\- Controlled reflections.

\- Natural material colors.

\- Balanced contrast between interior and exterior.



\## 6. Camera Presentation



The main visualization uses a wide panoramic composition.



Resolution: 3500 × 1080 px.



The camera should preserve natural proportions and avoid excessive distortion.



\## 7. Rendering Quality



Rendering priorities:



1\. Accurate geometry.

2\. Realistic materials.

3\. Physically plausible lighting.

4\. Natural reflections.

5\. Detailed textures.

6\. Controlled post-production.



\## 8. Visual Restrictions



Avoid:



\- Excessive decorative elements.

\- Unnecessary glossy surfaces.

\- Overexposed lighting.

\- Artificial-looking materials.

\- Excessive saturation.

\- Unrealistic proportions.



\## 9. Final Visual Goal



The final image should resemble high-end architectural photography.



The result must communicate architectural quality, comfort, exclusivity and a strong connection with the surrounding landscape.
