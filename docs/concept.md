

\# PROJECT CONCEPT

\## CINEMATIC MOUNTAIN RESIDENCE



\*\*Project:\*\* Cinematic Mountain Residence  

\*\*Author:\*\* Abdulla Hasanov  

\*\*Category:\*\* Interior Design / 3D Visualization  

\*\*Status:\*\* Concept Development



\---



\## 1. General Idea



The project represents a modern luxury mountain residence designed around a cinematic visual experience.



The main idea is to create an elegant, atmospheric and visually immersive interior that combines modern architecture, natural materials and panoramic views of the surrounding mountain landscape.



The interior should feel sophisticated, calm and connected to nature.



\## 2. Functional Organization



The main interior consists of three interconnected functional zones.



\### 2.1 Kitchen



The kitchen is positioned on the left side of the main composition.



Main design objectives:

\- Minimalist kitchen architecture.

\- Premium natural materials.

\- Dark wood and stone surfaces.

\- Integrated lighting.

\- Visually clean furniture arrangement.



\### 2.2 Lounge Area



The central zone connects the kitchen and living room.



Main design objectives:

\- Comfortable lounge furniture.

\- Strong visual connection with the exterior.

\- Decorative architectural elements.

\- Balanced lighting and materials.



\### 2.3 Living Room



The living room occupies the right side of the composition.



Main design objectives:

\- Comfortable seating arrangement.

\- Suspended fireplace as an architectural focal point.

\- Panoramic mountain views.

\- Warm and atmospheric lighting.



\## 3. Architectural Concept



The architecture should emphasize openness and visual continuity.



Key elements:



\- Floor-to-ceiling panoramic glazing.

\- Open-plan spatial organization.

\- Minimalist architectural geometry.

\- Integrated fireplace.

\- Carefully designed ceiling composition.

\- Connection between interior and exterior.



\## 4. Atmosphere



The intended atmosphere is cinematic, calm and sophisticated.



The interior should communicate:



\- Privacy.

\- Comfort.

\- Exclusivity.

\- Connection with nature.

\- Architectural elegance.



The lighting should create depth without making the interior excessively dark.



\## 5. Material Direction



Primary materials:



\- Natural stone.

\- Dark walnut.

\- Graphite surfaces.

\- Textured fabrics.

\- Bronze and dark metal.

\- Transparent and tinted glass.



Materials should have realistic surface properties and controlled reflections.



\## 6. Color Direction



| Color | HEX |

|---|---|

| Burgundy | #592B35 |

| Warm Light | #E8C99B |



The palette should maintain a balance between dark architectural surfaces and warm natural accents.



\## 7. Exterior Environment



The exterior environment is an important part of the visual composition.



Planned landscape elements:



\- Mountain slopes.

\- Forest-covered hills.

\- Tall golden grass.

\- Atmospheric sky.

\- Natural depth and perspective.



The landscape should complement the interior rather than compete with it.



\## 8. Visual Composition



The main camera should present all three functional zones within one panoramic frame.



The composition should emphasize:



\- Spatial depth.

\- Architectural proportions.

\- Furniture arrangement.

\- Panoramic glazing.

\- Relationship between interior and landscape.



Main resolution: 3500 × 1080 px.



\## 9. Technical Direction



Primary software:



\- Autodesk 3ds Max.

\- Corona Renderer.

\- Adobe Photoshop.



The production process will prioritize accurate geometry, realistic materials, physically plausible lighting and controlled post-production.



\## 10. Final Objective



Create a high-quality cinematic interior visualization that demonstrates professional skills in:



\- Interior design.

\- Architectural modeling.

\- Furniture modeling.

\- Material development.

\- Lighting.

\- Photorealistic rendering.

\- Visual presentation.



The completed project should be suitable for a professional design portfolio.
