
# CINEMATIC MOUNTAIN RESIDENCE

**Project Type:** Interior Design & 3D Visualization  
**Status:** Concept Development  
**Software:** Autodesk 3ds Max, Corona Renderer, Adobe Photoshop

---

## 01. Project Overview

Cinematic Mountain Residence is an experimental interior design project focused on creating a premium modern mountain residence.

The main objective is to develop a sophisticated architectural interior with a cinematic atmosphere, realistic materials, carefully designed lighting and panoramic mountain views.

## 02. Design Concept

The interior combines three functional zones into one continuous open-plan composition:

- Kitchen
- Lounge area
- Living room

The design is based on modern luxury, natural materials and architectural simplicity.
